Keep your board safe

Your board and local recovery snapshots are saved on this device. Use ONTO Settings → Export and save the JSON backup outside ONTO. Import restores that file on this or another device.

Install ONTO before building your everyday board. Browser and installed-app storage can be separate.

Share field-test feedback

Reply to the person who invited you. Include the ONTO version from Settings, your device and browser, what you did, what happened, and a screenshot when helpful.

Install ONTO

iPhone / iPad: Open ONTO in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App on when offered.

Mac: In Safari on macOS Sonoma or later, choose File → Add to Dock. Other browsers may offer an Install option; bookmarking ONTO also works.
